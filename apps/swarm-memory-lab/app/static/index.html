Why a shared map

Each drone only sees what is inside its sense radius. A hunter on the far wall would be blind to the prey without a radio. The map is that radio: whoever has eyes writes, and the scent decays so stale reports fade.

Three ways to learn

Isolated — two brains, empty map. The control. Blackboard — same two brains, plus the map. Hive — same two brains and the map; every hunter’s trajectory trains the hive, every prey’s trajectory trains prey.

Swarm logic

Roles are assigned from geometry: point, left flank, right flank, cutter. Reynolds separation keeps them from stacking. Coupling mixes those turns into the learned yaw. Turn it to zero to watch the net fly without a formation overlay.

Same physics as dogfight

Constant speed, yaw capped by speed ÷ turn radius, bullets that never steer. A sortie ends when every prey is down, the hive is wiped, or the clock runs out. Timeout with any prey still up is an escape.

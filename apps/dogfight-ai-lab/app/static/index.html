Why the turn radius matters

Instant heading changes would make this a pointing contest. A tight radius means every shot is bought with a curve. The dashed circles on the field are the only paths the plane is allowed to follow.

Last plane standing

There are no teams. A kill, a wall, or a mid-air takes you out. The fight continues until one aircraft is left, or timeout scores a draw.

One against the pack

P1 is chased. Everyone else hunts. The sortie ends when the chased plane dies, the pack is wiped, or the clock runs out. The pack wants a kill without losing hunters. The chased plane wants as many pack kills as it can take. Hunt stats are separate; hangar and library stay the same.

Revisions

Freeze a flying net with revise. The frozen parent lands in the library; the seat keeps a learning copy. Pick a library brain in the hangar copies it onto the plane. The library snapshot stays frozen. Only delete removes a revision. Swapping a seat without revising throws the old working copy away.
